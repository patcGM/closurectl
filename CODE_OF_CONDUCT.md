# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain professional, welcoming, and focused on improving
defensive security. Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving actionable, technically grounded feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- handling incident data and vulnerability reports responsibly.

Harassment, personal attacks, publication of private data, credential sharing,
or encouragement of unauthorized access are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
community chat, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious
security concerns should be reported through the private process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not through a public conduct report.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, proportionate to impact,
and applied consistently.
